Userscript examples for regex @include

Headers

  • An @include or @exclude wrapped in slashes, /.../, is a regular expression tested against the whole URL.
  • These variants try escaped and unescaped slashes inside it, an i flag after it, and case.

Details

  • Slashes inside a regex need no escaping.
  • A regex ignores case. A flag after the closing slash (/.../i) makes it no regex at all, so it matches nothing.
  • A glob ignores case too.
